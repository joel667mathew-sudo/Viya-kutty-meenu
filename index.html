A Letter to My Viya 💌

My Viya, my world,

Thank you for coming into my life when we were just two strangers on a chatting site. I never imagined that one conversation would lead me to someone who means this much to me.

Thank you for trusting me, for letting me know the real you, for being there when I am sad, and for letting me be there for you. Thank you for every late-night conversation, every laugh, every silly nickname, and every moment that became a memory I want to keep.

You are not only my hubby or my wife. You are my best friend, my master, my safe place, and my favourite person. I love your pure heart, your kindness, and all the little things that make you you.

We may have disagreements sometimes, but I promise to keep communicating, learning, and choosing us. I want to grow with you, support your dreams, celebrate your happiness, and hold your hand through the difficult days.

I want to spend my life with you—not only the big, magical moments, but the ordinary days too. More laughter, more cuddles, more adventures, more memories, and more years of being us.

Happy anniversary in advance, my love. October 23 is our special day, but every day with you is a gift.

Forever yours,
Your devoted wife ❤
